# Figure legends

## Fig. 1 Nitrogen-related functional profiles in the buffalo rumen

**a** Relative abundance of 20 nitrogen-related functional collections in all
30 animals, shown on a logarithmic abundance axis. **b** Diet-specific abundances
of glutamine synthetase (GS), glutamate synthase (GOGAT), glutamate dehydrogenase
(GDH) and urease structural genes. **c** Contributions of the three most abundant
KOs and the remaining KOs to the pooled nitrogen-metabolism collection;
K00262, K01915 and K00266 together account for 56.20%. **d** Relative abundance
of the nitrogen-metabolism collection across diets. Points represent individual
animals; boxes show the interquartile range and median on the plotted scale,
and whiskers extend to the most extreme values within 1.5 interquartile ranges.
There are six animals per diet. Relative abundance uses the all-gene abundance
denominator. The P and q values in d are from the initial-body-weight-adjusted
HC3 omnibus diet test, with Benjamini-Hochberg correction over 23 responses.
GS, GOGAT and GDH labels indicate annotated gene collections. BCAA denotes
branched-chain amino acids; amino acid abbreviations use standard three-letter
codes

## Fig. 2 Associations between carbohydrate-processing and nitrogen-related functions

**a** Partial correlations for all 80 pairs of 20 nitrogen-related collections
and four carbohydrate-processing collections. Starch denotes the combined
starch/alpha-glucan collection. Numbers indicate partial correlation coefficients.
Open circles indicate q < 0.05 in the permutation sensitivity analysis.
**b** Adjusted log-abundance residuals for GS and starch/alpha-glucan processing;
points indicate individual animals and the line is the fitted residual regression.
**c** Regression coefficients and HC3 95% confidence intervals for nitrogen
metabolism, GS, GOGAT and GDH versus starch/alpha-glucan processing.
Their HC3 q values are 0.128, 0.128, 0.182 and 0.128, respectively.
**d** Coefficient distributions from 30 leave-one-animal-out fits per pair;
open diamonds indicate the full-cohort estimates. Boxplot definitions follow
Fig. 1. Models adjust for diet, initial body weight and KO annotation load.
HC3 is the primary inference; within-diet residual permutation with 9,999
permutations is the sensitivity analysis. The two sets of q values are
Benjamini-Hochberg-adjusted separately over all 80 pairs

## Fig. S1 Principal component analysis of nitrogen-related functional profiles

**a** PC1 and PC2 scores for all 30 animals, colored and shaped by diet.
The analysis uses the natural-log abundances of 20 collections, centered and
scaled to unit standard deviation. **b** Variance explained by the first eight
components. **c** The 12 largest absolute PC1 loadings. Complete scores,
variance contributions and loadings are provided in the source tables

## Fig. S2 Complete pairwise diet contrasts

The ten panels show all pairwise diet contrasts for 23 responses, giving 230
points. The horizontal axis is the log2 adjusted abundance ratio for the first
diet relative to the second. The vertical axis is -log10 of the Benjamini-Hochberg
q value calculated across all 230 contrasts. The dashed line indicates q = 0.05.
Colors and symbols identify collection classes, rather than significance classes.
GS, GOGAT and GDH labels identify the prespecified enzyme collections.
Diet contrasts adjust for initial body weight

## Fig. S3 Individual nitrogen-related functional profiles

Heatmap of 20 functional collections across 30 animals. Values are within-feature
z scores of natural-log relative abundance. Animals are ordered by diet and then
animal identifier; each diet contains six animals. The complete 600-value matrix
is retained

## Fig. S4 Diet-specific distributions of all 20 functional collections

Points represent individual animals, with six animals per diet. Boxes and whiskers
follow Fig. 1. Panels have independent vertical ranges. Multiply the plotted
ordinate by the power-of-ten factor in the panel strip to recover relative
abundance in percent. Each q value comes from the original HC3 omnibus diet
test with Benjamini-Hochberg correction across all 23 responses, including
the three predefined ratios. All 600 animal-by-function values are shown
